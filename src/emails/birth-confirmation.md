Please arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment; late arrival will mean that you will not be seen and your appointment will be rebooked.

## Attendance
  - If you are legally married (or in a civil partnership) only one parent needs to attend
  - If you are NOT legally married (or in a civil partnership) both parents need to attend if you wish for both parents names to be included on the birth certificate

## Do not forget
You must bring proof of each parents' fullname, such as:
 - passport
 - UK or EU driving licence (full or provisional)
 - UK travel document
 - UK birth certificate
 - [sharecode for an eVisa](https://www.gov.uk/evisa/view-evisa-get-share-code-prove-immigration-status) or other biometric immigration document
 - EU identity card
 - [PASS Card](https://www.pass-scheme.org.uk/) or [digital PASS](https://www.pass-scheme.org.uk/pass-digital-page/)

If you do not have one of the documents above, you must bring two documents on the [secondary list].(https://www.leeds.gov.uk/births-deaths-and-marriages/register-a-birth). If you do not bring the correct documents the appointment will be rebooked.

## Certificate fees
Birth certificates cost £12.50 each. You can pay using a bank card or cash. If you bring cash, please have the exact amount.

## Interpreters
If you have requested an interpreter we will arrange one for you. If we are unable to arrange an interpreter for the appointment you have selected, we will contact you to offer a different date and time that the interpreter can attend.
